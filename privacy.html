My Universe — Privacy policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026
Developer: DraGon (Shymkent, Kazakhstan). Contact: bekarysss123456@gmail.com

1. What we collect

The developer does not collect any personal data. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

2. Location

If you allow it, the app reads your approximate (city-level) location. It is used only on your device to compute the positions and rise and set times of the Sun, the Moon and the planets. It is stored only on your device and is never sent to the developer or to anyone else. If you do not allow it, the app uses a default location.

3. Data on your device

Settings, favorites, recently viewed objects, exploration progress and cached sky images and texts are stored only on your device. They are deleted when you clear the app data or uninstall the app.

4. Internet connections

The app downloads sky images and object information from public scientific services: CDS Strasbourg (sky surveys and the name search), ESA (ESASky, ESA/Hubble and ESA/Webb images), the Wikimedia Foundation (Wikipedia, Wikidata) and NASA, and EOX IT Services (close-up satellite images of the Earth). As with any internet request, these servers receive your IP address and technical data of the request (for example the requested image or the object name you search for). They process this data under their own privacy policies. The app sends them no other information about you.

5. Purchases

The app is sold through Google Play. Payment is handled by Google; the developer never sees your card details. The developer only receives the order information that Google provides for refunds and taxes.

6. Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published in the app and on this page with a new effective date.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: bekarysss123456@gmail.com
